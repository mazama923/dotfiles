**Issue:** Repeating the mechanical grid inside search and content panels diluted the hierarchy and made the composition visually busy.

**Suggested improvement:** In Materials and depth, state that a decorative texture belongs to the canvas or a single structural layer; keep foreground panels materially calm unless the texture communicates information.

**Principle:** Texture establishes place; surfaces carry content. Repeating both on the same visual plane weakens each role.
